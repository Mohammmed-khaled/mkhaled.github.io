About Me

Computational Scientist

Computational scientist with over six years of experience at the intersection of scientific simulation, data analysis, computational biochemistry, and machine learning. Develops Python-based scientific software, including ATRANET and GeoMol++, and builds graph neural network–based generative models for molecular conformer prediction. Experienced in international, cross-institutional collaboration, with a strong track record of translating complex scientific problems into robust, reusable computational tools. Extensive experience working with large molecular datasets has developed strong skills in data wrangling, statistical analysis, visualization, and data-driven interpretation. Combines a solid foundation in Python programming, analytical problem-solving, and data storytelling with the ability to identify patterns and communicate meaningful insights. Now seeking to apply this technical and analytical background to broader data science and data-driven roles.
